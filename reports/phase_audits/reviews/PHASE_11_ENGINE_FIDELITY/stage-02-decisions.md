## Non-blocker

- **Decision 3 is correctly classified and that is the problem worth naming.** It is a poker
  rule with no oracle in this repo, and if it is wrong every later measurement that replays a
  hand inherits the error - the same damage a frozen artifact would do, arriving by a
  different road. The class system measures where a choice is written, not who can check it,
  so `frozen-into-data` would be the wrong label and using it to force a halt would be gaming
  the classifier. The record handles this the only honest way available: it flags decision 3
  at the top as the one call worth a human's eyes, states the rule in chips, and asks the
  question in one sentence a poker player can answer without reading code. The residual risk
  is real and is carried, not closed.
- **Decisions 7 and 8 are about documents, and neither class fits.** A contract amendment is
  neither runtime behaviour nor committed data. Both defaulted to `runtime-reversible`, which
  is the class that does not halt, and that is the right practical answer - a contract is
  amendable in `contract-update` mode by definition - but the vocabulary is being stretched.
  Worth knowing that the loop's two classes do not cover documentation decisions, rather than
  reading the label as a considered judgment about reversibility.
- **Decision 2 defers a real unknown to the build.** Whether any committed chart spot can
  return `fold` where `check` is legal has not been established, and the default is to find
  out by proving the property rather than by guarding against it. That is the right order -
  a guard would hide the finding - but it means stage 6 can surface a chart finding this
  phase may not fix, since the artifact belongs to proposed phase 14. If that happens the
  correct move is a backlog entry and a halt, not a guard added under time pressure.
- **The contract changed at this stage, which is unusual and is the measurement doing its
  job.** Two stage-1 criteria said things the probe contradicted: that the `street_bet >=
  to_call` guard makes the wrong reading fail loudly, and that the corrected producer changes
  the refusals in `latest_strategy_query_report.txt`. The guard misses a hero who has
  contributed exactly half the level, which is the heads-up small blind and therefore the
  exact case that motivated it; and that report runs the reference check-fold strategy, which
  has no refusals to change. Both criteria are corrected and the measured numbers are in the
  record. Flagged rather than left in the diff unexplained, because a contract edited at
  stage 2 is otherwise indistinguishable from a contract edited to suit a test.
- The contract is at 298 lines against a 300-line cap, unchanged from stage 1 despite the
  rewrite. Any further amendment buys its space by cutting, as the stage-1 note said.

- `STRATEGY-QUERY-STREET-BET-NAME` (filed this stage, proposed phase 13). Decision 4 keeps a

  guard instead. That is a deliberate trade against a blast radius of four completed phases'
  frozen tests, and it leaves the defect's root cause standing.
