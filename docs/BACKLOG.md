| PER-SEAT-CONTRIBUTIONS-IN-QUERY | deferred | strategy | Carry each seat's committed chips on StrategyQuery | StrategyQuery gives current stacks, the pot, and the price to call, but not what each seat has put in. Hero's own contribution is recoverable as street_bet minus to_call; nobody else's is. That blocks three things the preflop strategy currently approximates or misses. A short villain is indistinguishable from one who has already invested (ASYMMETRIC-EFFECTIVE-STACKS).
